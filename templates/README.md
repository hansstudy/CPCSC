# Templates

- [`gap-register.csv`](gap-register.csv): one row per ITSP.10.171 requirement with columns for status, owner, your organization-defined values, an evidence link, a target date, and notes. Open it in a spreadsheet and use it as the spine of your assessment.

Policies, plans, and logs are on hans.study: [14 free templates](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/) under CC BY 4.0, most buildable in the [policy builder](https://hans.study/tools/policy-builder/). They're listed with notes in [resources](../resources/hans-study.md#templates).
