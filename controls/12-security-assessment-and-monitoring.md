# 12 Security assessment and monitoring

4 requirements. Check that the controls work, plan and track the fixes, and keep watching.

What a first assessment tends to find: The gap register archived the day it was finished.

The work: Keep the Chapter 7 register alive, review it on a schedule, update it when the environment changes, and track the remediation tail to completion. This is where the system security plan and the register live.

## 03.12.01 Security assessment

Assess the system and its environment on a schedule to confirm the requirements are met.

Evidence to keep: self-assessment record, gap register, schedule.

You set: frequency. Record each value in your system security plan.

## 03.12.02 Plan of action and milestones

Keep a plan of action and milestones for known weaknesses and update it from assessments, audits, and monitoring.

Evidence to keep: POA&M with owners, milestones, and status.

[hans.study reading](https://hans.study/cpcsc/requirements/security-assessment-and-monitoring/#03.12.02) · [ITSP.10.171 text](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/protecting-specified-information-non-government-canada-systems-and-organizations-itsp10171)

- [ ] Met, with evidence filed

## 03.12.03 Continuous monitoring

Have a monitoring strategy that includes ongoing monitoring and assessments, and carry it out.

Evidence to keep: continuous monitoring plan, monitoring outputs, review records.

[hans.study reading](https://hans.study/cpcsc/requirements/security-assessment-and-monitoring/#03.12.03) · [ITSP.10.171 text](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/protecting-specified-information-non-government-canada-systems-and-organizations-itsp10171)

- [ ] Met, with evidence filed

## 03.12.05 Information exchange

Approve and manage exchanges of Specified Information with other systems under an agreement, and document the security responsibilities in it.

Evidence to keep: interconnection or information exchange agreements, approval records.

You set: frequency. Record each value in your system security plan.
