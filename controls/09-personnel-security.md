# 09 Personnel security

2 requirements. Screen people before they get access to protected data, and pull access cleanly when they leave or move roles.

What a first assessment tends to find: Hiring paperwork in a filing cabinet HR can't find, and the last engineer to leave still has a live account.

The work: Screening to the contract's requirements before enclave access, a named Company Security Officer where the contract requires one, and an offboarding process with a checklist that HR and IT both sign.

## 03.09.01 Personnel screening

Screen people before they get access to the system and rescreen them under conditions you define.

Evidence to keep: screening procedure, completion records, rescreening conditions.

You set: conditions requiring rescreening. Record each value in your system security plan.

## 03.09.02 Personnel termination and transfer

When someone leaves, disable access within a time you set, revoke credentials, and retrieve security-related property. When someone transfers, confirm they still need the access they have.

Evidence to keep: offboarding checklist with timestamps, transfer review records.

You set: time period. Record each value in your system security plan.
