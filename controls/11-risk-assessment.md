# 11 Risk assessment

3 requirements. Assess risk to the protected data periodically, scan for vulnerabilities on a cycle, and fix what the scans find inside a defined window.

What a first assessment tends to find: A penetration test bought before a single vulnerability scan was run, and a report nobody actioned.

The work: Scan the enclave on a cycle with proper tools, rank the findings, fix inside a defined window, and record the next scan date. Progress is what the assessor wants to see.

## 03.11.01 Risk assessment

Assess the risk of unauthorized disclosure from handling, processing, storing, or transmitting Specified Information, including supply chain risk, and update the assessment on a schedule.

Evidence to keep: risk assessment document, update dates, risk register.

You set: frequency. Record each value in your system security plan.

## 03.11.02 Vulnerability monitoring and scanning

Scan for vulnerabilities on a schedule and when new ones are announced, fix them within response times you define, and keep the scan content current.

Evidence to keep: scan reports, remediation tickets, defined response times.

You set: frequency, response times. Record each value in your system security plan.

## 03.11.04 Risk response

Act on findings from assessments, monitoring, and audits. A finding that sits unanswered is the failure this prevents.

Evidence to keep: finding tracker with owners, decisions, and dates.
