# 17 Supply chain risk management

3 requirements. Know who is in your supply chain for the in-scope systems, assess the risk they bring, and flow requirements down where protected data flows down.

What a first assessment tends to find: Subcontractors receiving drawings with no clause attached and no questions asked.

The work: An inventory of who touches in-scope systems and data, flow-down language in subcontracts, and evidence that the process repeats.

## 03.17.01 Supply chain risk management plan

Maintain a plan for supply chain risk across design, acquisition, delivery, operation, maintenance, and disposal, review it on a schedule, and protect it from disclosure.

Evidence to keep: supply chain risk management plan, review dates.

You set: frequency. Record each value in your system security plan.

## 03.17.02 Acquisition strategies, tools, and methods

Use acquisition strategies, contract tools, and procurement methods that identify and reduce supply chain risk.

Evidence to keep: procurement procedure, contract clauses, supplier assessment records.

## 03.17.03 Supply chain requirements and processes

Have a process to find and address weaknesses in your supply chain, and enforce the security requirements you've set to limit the harm from supply chain events.

Evidence to keep: supplier review process, requirements flowed down to suppliers, issue records.

You set: security requirements. Record each value in your system security plan.
