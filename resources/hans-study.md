## Templates

14 free templates under CC BY 4.0, sized for 12-to-45-computer shops. Each names the ITSP.10.171 requirements it helps satisfy and uses square brackets for the values you have to decide. Most can be built with your company's details in the [policy builder](https://hans.study/tools/policy-builder/).

| Template | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Level 1 requirements and evidence checklist](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/level-1-checklist/) | The 13 requirements with the evidence each one reaches for |
| [ITSP.10.171 requirement index](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/itsp-10-171-index/) | All 98 requirements as a checklist; the spine of a gap register |
| [Policy inventory checklist](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/policy-inventory/) | The 14 policies a small shop needs on paper |
| [Access control policy](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/access-control-policy/) | Role-based access, group hierarchy, and the review cadence |
| [Password and authentication policy](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/password-authentication-policy/) | One page, breach screening, MFA everywhere it counts |
| [Acceptable use policy](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/acceptable-use-policy/) | What stays off systems that hold Specified Information |
| [Change management policy and change request record](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/change-management/) | Configuration-controlled changes with approvals |
| [Incident response plan skeleton](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/incident-response-plan/) | With the contract reporting contacts built in |
| [Media handling policy and sanitization log](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/media-handling/) | Storage, transport, and destruction records |
| [Access review record](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/access-review-record/) | Who reviewed what, and when |
| [Offboarding checklist](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/offboarding-checklist/) | Access removal with timestamps |
| [Backup policy paragraph and restore test log](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/backup-and-restore-test/) | Backups, encryption, and proof they restore |
| [Shared responsibility matrix](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/shared-responsibility-matrix/) | Who operates, who holds evidence, what arrives when |
| [System security plan outline](https://hans.study/cpcsc/templates/system-security-plan-outline/) | The 9 sections an assessor expects |

## Services and contact

The practice reviews a supplier against the 13 Level 1 requirements and writes a plain-language gap list, and it supports CMMC and NIST SP 800-171 readiness on both the technical and documentation sides. See [CMMC 2.0 and CPCSC compliance](https://hans.study/defence-cmmc/), or write to [contact@hans.study](mailto:contact@hans.study).
